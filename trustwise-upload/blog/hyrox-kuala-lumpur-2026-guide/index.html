Check your race day first

Each division races on a specific day, and your wristband only works on that day. Confirm your day on the official HYROX site before booking flights or hotels. Several divisions are already sold out.

What is HYROX?

HYROX is a standardised fitness race, run in the same format all over the world. That means your time in KL is directly comparable to London, Hong Kong or New York. Every race is:

8 × 1 km runs, each followed by one workout station:

#Station
1SkiErg (1,000 m)
2Sled Push
3Sled Pull
4Burpee Broad Jumps
5Rowing (1,000 m)
6Farmers Carry
7Sandbag Lunges
8Wall Balls

Divisions: Open and Pro (heavier weights), raced Solo, as Doubles, or as a 4-person Relay. If it’s your first race, Open Doubles is the friendliest way to start.

Race-day tips from experienced HYROXers

  1. Pace the first run. Everyone goes out too fast, and you’ll pay for it at the sled push.
  2. Practise the Roxzone, the transition area between running and stations. Time is won and lost there.
  3. Wall balls come last and are where most people struggle. Break them into sets early.
  4. Bring: grippy shoes, gloves for the sled pull (optional), electrolytes and a change of clothes.
  5. Hydrate the day before. KL in December is hot and humid, even indoors.
  6. Arrive early to collect your wristband and walk the course.

Frequently asked questions

When is HYROX Kuala Lumpur 2026?

The official schedule runs over four days from Thursday 10 December 2026, and each division races on a set day. Some older listings show 12–13 December, so always check your assigned race day before booking travel.

Where is HYROX Malaysia held?

At MITEC (Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre), No. 8 Jalan Dutamas 2, KL Metropolis, Kuala Lumpur.

How much does HYROX Kuala Lumpur cost?

Entry started from about RM424 per person for the cheapest division and uses dynamic pricing, so it rises as places fill. Spectator tickets were about RM70 per day.

Can I watch HYROX on other days with my race wristband?

No. Your race wristband only gives venue access on your race day. You need a spectator ticket for any other day.
