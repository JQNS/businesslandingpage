Most people do Pattaya wrong. They land, stay on Beach Road, overpay for a jet-ski “deal” and go home thinking it’s just a party town.

The truth? Pattaya has some of the best day experiences in Thailand: a world-famous cabaret, one of Asia’s most impressive tropical gardens, a walk-through shark tunnel and dreamy floating cafés. All of them are within 30 minutes of your hotel.

Here’s how to do Pattaya like a pro: what’s worth your money, what to book ahead, and how to fit it all into two days.

2. Nong Nooch Tropical Garden: the one you can’t skip

If you only do one day trip in Pattaya, make it this one. Nong Nooch is a huge landscaped garden just south of the city, with a French-style garden, a cactus valley, dinosaur sculptures, a famous Stonehenge-style garden and a traditional Thai cultural show. It’s one of the most photogenic places in Thailand.

Pro tip: Go early in the morning before the heat and tour buses arrive, and wear comfortable shoes. The garden is bigger than you think.

Insider tips (read before you go)

  • Getting around: Use Grab (Southeast Asia’s Uber) for fixed, upfront prices. The blue “baht buses” (songthaews) are a cheap, fun way to get along Beach Road. Check the fare before you get on.
  • Skip the jet ski rentals: Scams over “damage” are well known. If you want to get on the water, use a reputable booked tour.
  • Cash vs card: Carry some baht for street food and songthaews. Pay for big-ticket items in advance online.
  • Dress code: Cover your shoulders and knees for temples such as the Sanctuary of Truth.
  • Stay connected: Get a Thailand eSIM before you fly so Grab and maps work as soon as you land.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Bangkok to Pattaya?

Pattaya is about 2 hours by road from Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK). A pre-booked private transfer is the easiest option, especially with luggage. Buses and taxis are cheaper but less convenient.

How many days do you need in Pattaya?

Two to three days is ideal. That's enough for Nong Nooch Garden, a show, an aquarium or café afternoon and some beach time without rushing.

Is it better to book Pattaya attractions online?

Yes. Booking ahead online usually costs less than walk-in prices, you skip the ticket queue, and many tickets are confirmed instantly on your phone.

What is the best time to visit Pattaya?

November to February is the cool, dry season and the most comfortable time to visit. March to May is very hot, and June to October brings rain showers.
