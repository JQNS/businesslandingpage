The easiest way to do Tomorrowland Thailand is the official hotel package. You get your festival pass, a hotel and transport in a single booking. Here’s what you get, how the hotel tiers work, and whether it’s better than booking everything yourself.

What’s included in a hotel package

According to the official Tomorrowland Thailand site, every hotel package includes:

🎟️Festival passFull Madness Pass (all 3 days)
🏨StayHotel near the festival
🍳FoodDaily breakfast
🚌TransportFree daily festival shuttle
🎁ExtrasSouvenir bag + Treasure Case

A comfort option for the Full Madness Pass is also available, which is Tomorrowland’s upgraded entry with better facilities.

Where are the hotels?

Package hotels are spread across three areas around the festival site at Wisdom Valley:

  • Pattaya: the most popular choice, with beaches, nightlife and plenty of food. Best if you want to explore between festival days.
  • Chonburi: often closer to the venue and quieter. Good if the festival is your main focus.
  • Rayong: further along the coast, more relaxed, and a good base for beach days after the weekend.

Bronze vs Silver vs Gold hotels

Hotels are grouped into three tiers so you can pick by budget and comfort:

TierBest for
🥉 BronzeBudget travellers and groups who mostly sleep at the hotel
🥈 SilverA good balance of comfort and price
🥇 GoldPremium hotels, ideal for recovery days by the pool
Prices

Package prices depend on the hotel, room type and number of people. Check the latest prices and availability on the official site, as they change often.

The daily festival shuttle: the real advantage

On festival nights, tens of thousands of people leave Wisdom Valley at the same time. Package holders get free daily shuttles with:

  • Less traffic: dedicated routes to and from the festival.
  • The closest pick-up and drop-off points to the festival entrance.

For many people, this alone makes the package worth it. You won’t be fighting for a Grab at 1am.

Our take: If you don’t have a pass yet, the package may be your best route in, so check the official site for any remaining availability. If you already have a pass, booking your own hotel usually gives you more choice and better value.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a Tomorrowland Thailand hotel package?

Hotel accommodation, daily breakfast, a 3-day Full Madness Pass, free daily shuttles to and from the festival, a Tomorrowland souvenir bag and a Treasure Case.

Where are the Tomorrowland Thailand package hotels?

In Pattaya, Chonburi and Rayong, grouped into Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers.

Is the Tomorrowland Thailand hotel package worth it?

If you do not have a festival pass yet, a package is one of the few official ways to get one. If you already have a pass, booking your own hotel usually gives more choice.
