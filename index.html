Good to Know

Common questions, quick answers.

How does selling my cards work?

WhatsApp us clear photos of your cards (front and back) and we'll give you a quote. Happy with the offer? Come in to Amcorp Mall or arrange a meetup. Walk out with cash on the spot.

How does consignment work?

Drop off your cards with us. We'll list them at a price you agree on. When they sell, we deduct a small commission and pay you the rest. Great for high-value cards where you don't want to undersell.

Do you buy graded / PSA slabs?

Yes! We buy and sell PSA, BGS and CGC graded cards. WhatsApp us the cert number and grade and we'll get back with an offer.

What sets or cards are you currently buying?

We're always buying across all generations — Base Set, modern sets, Japanese exclusives, promos and more. If you're unsure, just ask — we'll be honest about whether we're interested.

Can I trade cards instead of selling for cash?

Absolutely. Bring your trade binder in-store and we'll work out a value-for-value deal. We trade fairly and don't low-ball on trade value.

Do you have sealed products like booster boxes?

Yes, we stock sealed product — booster boxes, elite trainer boxes, tins and more. Stock changes regularly so WhatsApp us to check availability.

Where exactly are you in Amcorp Mall?

We're inside Amcorp Mall, Petaling Jaya. WhatsApp us before you visit and we'll share the exact unit location and any current parking tips.
