Why Racket Connect

Coaching that grows with your child — at a price that makes sense.

We keep things simple: clear progression, supportive coaches, and class sizes small enough that every kid actually gets time on court. No flashy promises — just steady improvement and a lot of fun along the way.

Affordable, transparent fees

Class packages are priced for everyday families — no surprise charges, no hard-sell. Pay per term or per session, whichever works for you.

Small groups, real attention

We cap groups at 4–8 students so coaches can actually watch each player, fix technique early, and keep practice engaging.

Progress you can see

Beginner, foundation, and competitive tracks. Every term we share a short progress note so parents know what their child is working on.

Safe & encouraging space

Friendly coaches, clear rules, warm-ups before every session. We want kids to feel welcome — especially the shy first-timers.

Flexible time slots

Weekday evenings and weekend mornings across all three locations. Easy for school-going kids and busy parents.

Free trial class

Good to know

Quick questions, quick answers.

Do you offer a trial class?

Yes. New students can book a trial session so you can see if it's a good fit before signing up for a term.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable sports attire, court shoes (non-marking), a water bottle, and a racket if you have one. We can lend a spare racket for trial sessions.

How are classes structured?

Each session includes warm-up, drills focused on a specific skill, mini-games or match practice, and a cool-down. We rotate focus areas weekly.

How is pricing different from market rates?

We keep overheads lean and pass the savings on to families. Term packages are designed to be friendlier on the wallet without cutting coaching quality.

Can I switch locations or class times mid-term?

Where possible, yes — just let us know early and we'll try to fit you into a suitable slot.
