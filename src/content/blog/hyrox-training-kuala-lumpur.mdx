## Official HYROX Training Club: Fitness First

**Fitness First** is an official HYROX Training Club in Malaysia. Its HYROX classes run at clubs across KL and Selangor:

| Area | Clubs |
|---|---|
| KL city | The Gardens Mall, Avenue K, NU Empire |
| Petaling Jaya and Subang | The Curve, Setia City Mall |
| South and east | IOI Mall Puchong, Cheras LeisureMall, Melawati Mall |
| Klang | AEON Mall Bukit Tinggi |

**Five class types**, so you can build up to race pace:

1. **HYROX Foundational**: the basics and a fitness baseline
2. **HYROX Engine**: aerobic capacity
3. **HYROX Power**: explosive power and functional strength
4. **HYROX Complete**: race-day movements and transitions
5. **HYROX Perform**: a 17-week programme to prepare for a race

Non-members can register for a **complimentary HYROX class**, and you don't need a race ticket to join. Source: [Fitness First Malaysia](https://www.fitnessfirst.com/my/en/HYROX-Official-Training-Club).

## Other KL gyms with HYROX-style training

These independent gyms advertise HYROX or HYROX-style conditioning classes. Not all of them are official HYROX Training Clubs, so ask about the equipment (SkiErg, sleds, rowers, wall balls) before you sign up.

| Gym | Area | What they offer |
|---|---|---|
| **MORPH by Kamileon** | Bukit Bintang (Tuah 1895 @ BBCC), Bukit Kiara (KLGCC) | HYROX classes |
| **Babel HYROX Training Ground** | TTDI (Menara Ken rooftop) | HYROX-focused training |
| **Afterburn Training Club** | Damansara Heights (Plaza Damansara) | "HYROX Engine" conditioning class |

Gyms change their timetables often. Check each gym's latest schedule before you go.

## Try before you commit

A gym membership is a big commitment when you don't know yet if you like the coaching, the equipment or the crowd. A class-booking membership lets you try several gyms in a month and keep the one that fits.

## A simple 8-week plan for your first HYROX

This is a general plan for healthy adults who already exercise. If you're new to training or have an injury, talk to a coach or doctor first.

| Weeks | Focus | Each week |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | Base | 2 easy runs, 2 full-body strength sessions, 1 class to learn the stations |
| **3–4** | Stations | Practise 4 stations per session with short runs between them |
| **5–6** | Compromised running | 1 km runs straight after sleds, lunges and wall balls: the hardest part of race day |
| **7** | Simulation | One half or full race simulation at race pace |
| **8** | Taper | Lighter week, sleep, stay healthy, check your race day and kit |

<Callout type="tip" title="The station everyone underestimates">
Wall balls come last, when your legs are already gone. Practise them tired, at the end of a session, not fresh at the start.
</Callout>
