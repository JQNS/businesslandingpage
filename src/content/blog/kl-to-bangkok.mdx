For most Malaysians, getting to Bangkok means a 2-hour flight. The real decisions are **which airport**, **when to book**, and what to sort out before you land. And if you have time, there's a classic overland route by train.

## Option 1: Fly (what almost everyone does)

| | Budget airlines (e.g. AirAsia) | Full-service (e.g. Malaysia Airlines) |
|---|---|---|
| Usual Bangkok airport | Don Mueang (DMK) | Suvarnabhumi (BKK) |
| Checked bag | Usually paid add-on | Usually included |
| Meals | Paid | Included |
| Best for | Light packers, flexible dates | Families, shoppers with big bags |

**How to pay less:**

- **Compare the total**, not the headline fare. Add a checked bag both ways: Bangkok shopping means you'll need it on the way home.
- **Add baggage online** before the flight. Adding it at the airport counter costs much more.
- **Avoid peak dates** if you can: Malaysian school holidays, long weekends, Songkran and New Year.
- **Fly mid-week.** Tuesday and Wednesday departures are often cheaper than Friday.

<Callout type="tip" title="Check your Bangkok airport">
DMK and BKK are about an hour apart. Check your booking, then see how to get from [your airport to the city](/blog/bangkok-airport-to-city/).
</Callout>

## Option 2: The train (for the journey, not the savings)

It's a two-day adventure, not a shortcut:

1. **KTM ETS from KL Sentral to Padang Besar**: about 6 hours.
2. **Immigration at Padang Besar station**: both Malaysian and Thai checks are inside the station.
3. **Thai overnight sleeper train to Bangkok**: departs Padang Besar in the late afternoon (around 17:00 Thai time), arrives at **Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal** around 09:00 the next morning. A sleeper berth costs roughly 850–920 baht.

Lower berths sell out first in busy periods. Book ahead, and check current timetables with KTMB and the State Railway of Thailand ([timetable source](https://www.train36.com/padang-besar-to-bangkok-train.html)).

**Who should do it:** travellers with time who want the experience. You can also break the trip with a night in [Hatyai](/blog/kl-to-hatyai/).
