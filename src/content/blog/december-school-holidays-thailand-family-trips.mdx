The year-end school holidays are almost a month long this year, and December is Thailand's best weather. That's why flights and hotels fill up fast. If you're taking the kids to Thailand, **book now**, not in late November.

Here are the dates, plus five Thailand trips that actually work with children.

## The 5 trips at a glance

| Trip | Days | Best for | Getting there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hatyai** | 2–3 | Northern families, first trip abroad with kids | Drive, train or short flight |
| **2. Bangkok** | 4–5 | Shopping, malls, Safari World | Direct flights from KL, Penang, JB |
| **3. Pattaya** | 3–4 | Beach plus attractions, bigger kids | Bangkok airport + 2 hrs by road |
| **4. Chiang Mai** | 4–5 | Cool weather, elephants, nature | Direct flights from KL |
| **5. Phuket or Krabi** | 4–6 | Beaches and island hopping | Direct flights from KL |

## 1. Hatyai: the easy first trip

Hatyai is the classic Malaysian family trip: close, cheap, great food and shopping. Families from Kedah, Penang and Perlis can drive through Bukit Kayu Hitam. Others can fly or take the train.

**Kids will like:** the markets, the cable car at Hat Yai Municipal Park, and lots of snacks.

**Driving in?** Every passenger, including kids, still needs a [TDAC arrival card](/blog/tdac-thailand-digital-arrival-card-malaysians/), and your car needs Thai insurance at the border. Expect long queues at the checkpoint at the start of school holidays, so cross early in the morning.

## 2. Bangkok: malls, markets and Safari World

Bangkok works for every age. Malls are air-conditioned escapes from the afternoon heat, and the BTS and MRT are stroller-friendly at most stations.

**Kids will like:** Safari World, Dream World, SEA LIFE Bangkok, and the river boats.

Plan your days with our [3-day Bangkok itinerary](/blog/bangkok-3-day-itinerary/), and see [how to get from the airport to your hotel](/blog/bangkok-airport-to-city/). With kids and luggage, a pre-booked transfer is worth it.

## 3. Pattaya: beach and attractions

Pattaya mixes beach time with big family attractions: Nong Nooch Garden, Underwater World and the floating cafés. Read [Pattaya like a pro](/blog/things-to-do-in-pattaya/) for what's worth booking.

<Callout type="warn" title="Tomorrowland weekend: 11–13 December">
Tomorrowland Thailand takes place near Pattaya on 11–13 December 2026. Hotels will be busier and pricier that weekend. Families should plan Pattaya before or after it. More in our [Tomorrowland Thailand guide](/blog/tomorrowland-thailand-2026-guide/).
</Callout>

## 4. Chiang Mai: cool weather and nature

December is Chiang Mai's coolest month, a real change from Malaysian heat. Mornings can be chilly in the mountains, so pack a jacket.

**Kids will like:** ethical elephant sanctuaries (choose ones with no riding), the night markets and Doi Inthanon, Thailand's highest mountain.

Going in late November instead? You'll catch the lantern festival. See [Loy Krathong & Yi Peng 2026](/blog/loy-krathong-yi-peng-2026-chiang-mai/).

## 5. Phuket or Krabi: beaches

December is peak season on the Andaman coast, with calm seas and clear water for island hopping. Krabi (Ao Nang) is quieter and more relaxed. Phuket has more choice of hotels and activities.

**With small kids:** choose a hotel with a pool on a calm beach, and keep boat trips short.
