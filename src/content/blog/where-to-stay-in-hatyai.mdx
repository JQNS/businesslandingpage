## Driving up from Malaysia?

- **Filter for parking** on Agoda, and check that it's free and on site. Hatyai's city centre streets are busy and narrow.
- **Arrive before the evening rush** on Friday. The Bukit Kayu Hitam–Sadao border can have long queues on long weekends.
- Read our [KL to Hatyai guide](/blog/kl-to-hatyai/) for the train, bus and driving options, plus border tips.

<Callout type="tip" title="Long weekend? Book early">
On Malaysian school holidays and long weekends, central Hatyai hotels fill up and prices jump. Book early with free cancellation, then rebook if you find a better deal.
</Callout>

## Halal food in Hatyai

Hatyai has a large Muslim community, so halal food is easy to find around the city centre and the markets. Look for the **green Thai halal sign** on restaurants and food stalls.
