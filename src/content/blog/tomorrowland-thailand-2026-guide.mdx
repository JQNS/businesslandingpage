If you missed out, use the **official waiting list in your account on tomorrowland.com**. Don't buy from Facebook, Telegram or Carousell sellers — tickets are tied to the buyer's name and fakes are common.

## How to get there from Malaysia

**1. Fly KL → Bangkok (BKK or DMK).** Flights take about 2 hours. Book early: festival week prices climb fast.

**2. Bangkok → Pattaya.** Your options:
- **Private transfer** (easiest with a group and luggage, ~1.5–2 hours)
- **Airport bus** from BKK to Pattaya (cheapest)
- **Grab/taxi** (agree on a price or use the meter)

## Where to stay for Tomorrowland Thailand

<Callout type="tip" title="Want everything in one booking?">
The official hotel packages bundle your 3-day pass, hotel and daily shuttle. See our <a href="/blog/tomorrowland-thailand-hotel-packages/">Tomorrowland Thailand hotel packages guide</a>.

Most festival-goers stay in **Pattaya**, which has the most hotels and nightlife and is about 30 minutes from the venue. Book early, because festival weekend rooms sell out and prices jump.

### Best areas
- **Central Pattaya / Beach Road:** walkable, lots of food, easy Grab pickups.
- **Jomtien:** quieter and cheaper, good for groups.
- **Near the venue (Chonburi side):** shorter drive, but fewer hotels and less to do.

## Money, SIM and other tips

- **Cash:** Bring some Thai baht for taxis and street food. Festival payments may be cashless, so check the official app.
- **SIM/eSIM:** Get a Thai eSIM before you fly so you're online as soon as you land. You'll need data for Grab, maps and the festival app.
- **Visa:** Malaysians can visit Thailand visa-free for short stays. Check the current rules and entry form requirements before you travel.
- **Travel insurance:** Strongly recommended for a festival trip abroad.
