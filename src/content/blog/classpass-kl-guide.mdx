## How ClassPass works

1. **Pick a monthly plan.** Each plan gives you a set number of credits. Bigger plans give you more credits.
2. **Find a class.** Search by area, time or type: spin, HIIT, strength, boxing, yoga, reformer Pilates, dance and more.
3. **Book with credits.** Every class shows its credit price. Popular studios and peak times cost more credits. Quieter times cost fewer.
4. **Show up and check in.** The studio has your booking. Just give your name at the front desk.

Prices change from time to time, so check the current Malaysian plans in the app or on the sign-up page before you choose. Start small if you're not sure. You can change your plan later.

## The rules that catch new members out

### Credits roll over, but not all of them

On an active paid monthly plan, unused credits you've paid for **roll over, up to the number of credits in your next month's plan**. Trial and free credits usually **don't** roll over. If you cancel your membership, any credits left are lost at the end of the billing cycle.

### The 12-hour cancellation window

You can usually cancel a class **up to 12 hours before** it starts with no fee, and your credits come back. Cancel later than that, or don't show up, and you'll pay a **late cancellation or no-show fee**. Some studios set an earlier deadline, so check the class details when you book.

<Callout type="warn" title="Set a reminder">
Most late cancellation fees happen with morning classes booked the night before. If you book a 7am class, decide by 7pm the evening before.
</Callout>

### It's month to month

There's no annual contract. Cancel before your next billing date if you don't want another month.

## 5 ways to get more out of your credits

1. **Book off-peak.** Mid-morning and early-afternoon classes often cost fewer credits than 7am and 7pm slots.
2. **Mix expensive and cheap classes.** Save your credits for the reformer studio you love, and use cheaper strength or yoga classes for the rest of the week.
3. **Use your rollover.** Had a busy month? Unused paid credits carry forward (up to your plan's limit), so you don't have to cram classes in.
4. **Try a studio before buying its package.** If you find one you'd go to 4+ times a week, buying that studio's own package may work out cheaper.
5. **Book early for popular classes.** The best reformer and spin slots in Bangsar and Mont Kiara fill up days ahead.

## Is ClassPass worth it?

**ClassPass is a good fit if you:**
- Get bored doing the same workout every week
- Want to try reformer Pilates, spin or HYROX-style training before committing to one studio
- Work in one area and live in another, and want classes near both
- Are training for an event, like [HYROX Kuala Lumpur](/blog/hyrox-kuala-lumpur-2026-guide/), and want different kinds of sessions
- Need indoor options during the haze (see [haze workouts in KL](/blog/haze-indoor-workouts-kl/))

**A regular gym membership is better if you:**
- Train at the same gym 5+ times a week
- Mostly do your own programme with free weights
- Want 24-hour access
