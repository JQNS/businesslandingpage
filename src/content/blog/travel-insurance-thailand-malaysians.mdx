## Do you really need it for Thailand?

It's not required for a visa-free holiday, but here's why it's worth it:

- **Private hospitals in Bangkok, Phuket and Pattaya are excellent, but expensive** for tourists. A few nights in hospital can cost far more than your whole trip.
- **Your Malaysian medical card or company panel often doesn't work overseas.** Check before you assume.
- **Flights get cancelled and delayed**, especially in the monsoon and around festivals.
- **Tourist hotspots have pickpockets.** Crowded markets, festivals and nightlife areas are where phones and wallets disappear.

## What a good plan covers

| Cover | Why it matters in Thailand | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Overseas medical** | Private hospital bills | Malaysian plans often offer RM250,000 to RM2 million |
| **Emergency evacuation** | Getting you home or to a better hospital | Included, with a high limit |
| **Trip cancellation** | Illness or emergencies before you go | Malaysian plans often offer RM2,500 to RM12,500 |
| **Flight delay / missed connection** | Budget-airline delays | Pays out after a set number of hours |
| **Baggage and personal items** | Lost bags, stolen phones | Per-item limits (phones often have a low cap) |
| **Personal liability** | Accidentally injuring someone or damaging property | Included |

## The fine print that catches Malaysians out

1. **Scooters and motorbikes.** Riding without a valid **motorcycle licence** (not just a car licence) or without a helmet is a common reason claims are rejected.
2. **Alcohol.** Injuries while drunk are often excluded. Think Full Moon parties, Songkran and Walking Street.
3. **Festivals and adventure activities.** Diving, ziplining, ATVs and some water sports may need an add-on. Check if you're going to [Tomorrowland](/blog/tomorrowland-thailand-2026-guide/) or [Erawan Falls](/blog/day-trips-from-bangkok/).
4. **Pre-existing conditions.** Usually excluded unless you declare them and the insurer accepts them.
5. **Theft with no police report.** You'll usually need a police report from the Thai Tourist Police (call **1155**) within 24 hours.
6. **Unattended items.** A phone left on a beach chair is usually not covered.

<Callout type="warn" title="Keep the paperwork">
For any claim, keep receipts, hospital reports, police reports and the airline's delay letter. Take photos on your phone as you go. Claims without documents are often rejected.
</Callout>

## Where to buy

- **Insurers in Malaysia:** many Malaysian general insurers and takaful operators sell single-trip and annual travel plans online.
- **Comparison sites:** sites like RinggitPlus let you compare medical limits, cancellation cover and price side by side.
- **Airline add-on:** convenient at checkout, but compare the cover first. It's not always the best value.
- **Credit card cover:** a nice bonus, but often basic and may not include overseas medical. Don't rely on it alone.

**Travelling more than twice a year?** An **annual plan** is often cheaper than buying single-trip cover each time, especially if you do Hatyai, Bangkok and Singapore weekends.

**Muslim travellers:** many providers have **takaful** travel plans with similar cover.

## How to choose in 10 minutes

1. Pick plans with **at least RM250,000 overseas medical cover** and emergency evacuation.
2. Check **trip cancellation**, matching the amount you've paid for flights and hotels.
3. Read the **exclusions**, especially scooters, alcohol and activities you plan to do.
4. Compare **phone and gadget limits** if you're bringing expensive gear.
5. Buy it **the day you book** so cancellation cover starts straight away.

## Other ways to protect your trip

- **Book hotels with free cancellation** so plans can change without losing money. See [where to stay in Bangkok](/blog/where-to-stay-in-bangkok/).
- **Get a local eSIM** so you can call your insurer's 24-hour helpline, use maps and book Grab safely.
- **Keep your passport safe** and carry a photo of it on your phone.
