The most important Bangkok hotel decision isn't stars or room size. It's **location**. Stay within walking distance of a BTS or MRT station and Bangkok is easy. Stay in the wrong place and you'll spend your holiday stuck in traffic.

## Bangkok areas at a glance

| Area | Best for | Train | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sukhumvit (Asok / Phrom Phong)** | First-timers, food, nightlife | BTS + MRT | Busy, modern, lots of choice |
| **Nana** | Halal food, budget | BTS Nana | Lively, a bit rough at night |
| **Siam** | Malls, families | BTS Siam / National Stadium | Central and easy |
| **Pratunam** | Shopping on a budget | Airport Rail Link, short walk to BTS | Crowded, wholesale markets |
| **Silom / Sathorn** | Value, business trips | BTS + MRT | Calmer at night |
| **Riverside** | Views, couples | BTS Saphan Taksin + boats | Relaxed, resort-like |
| **Old Town / Banglamphu** | Temples, backpackers | MRT Sanam Chai, boats | Historic, near Khao San Road |

## Sukhumvit (Asok): best all-rounder

Asok is where the BTS and MRT lines cross, so you can get almost anywhere. Terminal 21 mall's food court is one of the cheapest good meals in the city.

## Nana: best for halal food on a budget

The streets around Sukhumvit Soi 3 to 5, known as **Soi Arab**, are packed with Middle Eastern, Indian and Malay-friendly halal restaurants open late. The area gets lively at night, so families may prefer Siam.

## Pratunam: best for shopping on a budget

Home to **Platinum Fashion Mall** and Pratunam Market, the place for wholesale clothes. It's crowded and traffic is bad, but you can shop until you drop and walk back to your room.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pratunam tip">
Pratunam isn't right on the BTS. The nearest stations (Chit Lom and Ratchathewi) are a 10–15 minute walk, and the Airport Rail Link stops at Ratchaprarop. Shoppers don't mind. Sightseers may prefer Siam.
</Callout>

The business district is quieter at night, with good-value hotels and easy access to the BTS, MRT and Lumpini Park.

## Riverside and the Old Town

- **Riverside:** great views, calmer evenings and river boats to the Grand Palace. Best for couples and a slower trip.
- **Old Town / Banglamphu:** closest to the Grand Palace, Wat Pho and Khao San Road. It's further from the malls, but full of character.
