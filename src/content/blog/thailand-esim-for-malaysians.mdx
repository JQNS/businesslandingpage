## Step by step: set up your eSIM

1. **Check your phone.** Dial `*#06#`. If an EID shows, you're good. Your phone must not be telco-locked.
2. **Buy the eSIM** for the number of days you'll be in Thailand. Count your arrival and departure days.
3. **Install it the day before you fly**, on home Wi-Fi. Scan the QR code from your booking (iPhone: Settings → Mobile Service → Add eSIM; Samsung: Settings → Connections → SIM manager → Add eSIM).
4. **Label the lines** "MY" and "TH" so you don't mix them up.
5. **When you land:** set the TH eSIM as your mobile data line and turn on data roaming *for the eSIM only*. Turn **off** data roaming on your Malaysian line.
6. **Keep your Malaysian line on** for calls and SMS. You'll still get bank OTPs.

<Callout type="warn" title="Avoid a surprise roaming bill">
If your Malaysian line still has data roaming on, your phone may use it instead of the eSIM. Double-check that your Malaysian line's data roaming is off before you leave the airport.
</Callout>

## When roaming or an airport SIM makes more sense

- **Day trip or overnight in Hatyai or Danok:** your telco's roam pass may be simpler.
- **Your phone doesn't support eSIM:** buy a tourist SIM at the airport, or a physical SIM before you fly.
- **Staying a month:** a local Thai prepaid SIM can work out cheapest.
