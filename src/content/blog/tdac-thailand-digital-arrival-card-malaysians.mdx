Going to Thailand soon? There's one form you can't skip: the **TDAC (Thailand Digital Arrival Card)**. It replaced the old paper TM6 card in May 2025, it takes about 5 minutes, and it's **free**.

Here's exactly how Malaysians fill it in, whether you're flying from KLIA or driving up through Bukit Kayu Hitam.

<Callout type="warn" title="Watch out for fake TDAC sites">
Search "TDAC" and you'll see sites that look official and charge a fee to "process" your card. The real form is free and only on **tdac.immigration.go.th** (note the **.go.th**). If a site asks for payment, close it.

## Who needs a TDAC?

Every foreign passport holder entering Thailand, including Malaysian tourists, business travellers and long-term residents. Children need one too.

You **don't** need one if:

- You're transiting through a Thai airport without passing immigration, or
- You're entering with a **Border Pass**.

<Callout type="info" title="TDAC is not a visa">
The TDAC is only an arrival form. Malaysians can visit Thailand visa-free for tourism, but the rules changed in September 2026. Read our [Thailand entry rules for Malaysians](/blog/thailand-entry-rules-malaysians/) guide before you book.
</Callout>

## What you need before you start

Have these ready and the form takes 5 minutes:

- **Passport:** number, expiry date, nationality
- **Flight or travel details:** flight number and arrival date, or for drivers, your vehicle and border checkpoint
- **Where you're staying:** your hotel name and address in Thailand (your booking confirmation works)
- **Email address:** your confirmation and QR code are sent here
- **Recent travel history:** the form includes a short health declaration about countries you've visited

## Step-by-step: how to fill in the TDAC

1. **Open the official site** at tdac.immigration.go.th within the 3 days before you arrive.
2. **Choose "Arrival Card"** and fill in your personal details exactly as they appear in your passport.
3. **Add your trip details:** arrival date, mode of travel (air, land or sea), flight number or border point, and purpose (Holiday).
4. **Enter your accommodation** in Thailand. If you're moving between cities, use your first hotel.
5. **Complete the health declaration.**
6. **Add other travellers** if you're submitting for family or friends.
7. **Submit and save the QR code.** Screenshot it and keep the confirmation email. Immigration may scan it on arrival.

**Pro tip:** Do it the night before your flight, not at the airport. Airport Wi-Fi and a queue behind you are a bad combination.

## Flying vs driving in from Malaysia

| | Flying (KLIA, Penang, JB) | Driving or bus (Bukit Kayu Hitam, Padang Besar) |
|---|---|---|
| TDAC needed? | Yes | Yes (unless using a Border Pass) |
| Travel mode on form | Air + flight number | Land + checkpoint |
| Extra for drivers | — | Your car also needs Thai vehicle paperwork and Thai insurance at the border |
| Common mistake | Wrong arrival date after a delay | Forgetting the form for passengers in the car |

If your flight changes, you can edit your TDAC on the same site using your reference number.
