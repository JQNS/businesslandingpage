Bangkok is the most popular overseas trip for Malaysians, and it's easy to waste it stuck in traffic between the wrong sights.

This plan groups each day by area, so you spend your time at the temples, markets and malls, not in a Grab. It works for a long weekend or the first three days of a longer Thailand trip.

## Day 1: Temples and the river

**Morning: Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew.** Go when it opens to beat the heat and the tour groups. Follow the dress code or you'll be sent to buy cover-ups at the gate.

**Late morning: Wat Pho.** A short walk away. See the giant Reclining Buddha, and if your legs are tired, the temple's massage school is famous for Thai massage.

**Afternoon: Wat Arun.** Take the cross-river ferry from Tha Tien pier, a few minutes' ride. Wat Arun looks its best in late afternoon light.

**Evening: Chinatown (Yaowarat).** Street food gets going after dark. Muslim travellers: Bangkok has many halal restaurants. Look for the Thai halal logo, or ask your hotel for the nearest options.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro move: use the river boat">
The Chao Phraya Express Boat connects the old town, Wat Arun, Chinatown and ICONSIAM. It's faster than a car in traffic, cheap, and the views are a bonus.
</Callout>

## Day 2: Floating market, railway market and the river at night

**Morning to afternoon: Damnoen Saduak Floating Market and Maeklong Railway Market.** Both are 1.5–2 hours outside Bangkok, so a tour is the easiest way. At Maeklong, vendors pull back their awnings minutes before a train passes through the market. A tour times your visit for it.

## Day 3: Shop like a Malaysian

**If it's Saturday or Sunday: Chatuchak Weekend Market.** Thousands of stalls of clothes, home items and snacks. Go in the morning before it gets hot, and take the MRT or BTS.

**If it's a weekday: Platinum Fashion Mall and Pratunam.** Wholesale-price clothing, and many stalls give a discount if you buy several pieces.

**Afternoon: Siam.** Siam Paragon, Siam Center and CentralWorld are connected by skywalk.

**Night:** a night market such as Jodd Fairs for food and photos.

<Callout type="warn" title="Leave room in your bag">
