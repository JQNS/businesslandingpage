## The best countdown spots

| Spot | Vibe | Best for | Nearest transport |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICONSIAM** | River fireworks, big show | Fireworks lovers, families | Gold Line, river boat |
| **CentralWorld** | Massive street party, concerts | Groups of friends, atmosphere | BTS Chit Lom / Siam |
| **One Bangkok** | Newer venue, light and laser shows | Something different | MRT Lumphini |
| **Asiatique** | Riverside, Ferris wheel | Couples, river views | Free shuttle boat from Saphan Taksin |
| **Dinner cruise** | Fireworks from the water | Couples, special occasions | River piers |

### ICONSIAM: river fireworks
ICONSIAM's "Amazing Thailand Countdown" is known for a long fireworks show over the Chao Phraya River, with drones in recent years. Riverbanks on both sides fill up early, so arrive by late afternoon for a good spot.

### CentralWorld: the "Times Square of Asia"
The crossroads at Ratchaprasong turns into one huge street concert. Last year's countdown drew more than 350,000 people. It's loud and packed, and fun if you like crowds. Keep your phone and wallet zipped in front of you.

### One Bangkok and Asiatique
Newer and riverside alternatives with smaller crowds than CentralWorld. Good if you want a show without being shoulder to shoulder.

## Watch from the river

A dinner cruise on New Year's Eve gives you fireworks from the water and no crowds. NYE cruises have special pricing and sell out early, so check availability as soon as dates open.

## Where to stay for New Year's Eve

Stay within walking distance of where you'll count down. After midnight, stations are packed and Grab prices surge.

| Area | Walk to | Why stay here |
|---|---|---|
| **Riverside (Charoen Nakhon, Saphan Taksin)** | ICONSIAM, Asiatique | River views, fireworks from some hotel rooms |
| **Ratchaprasong / Siam** | CentralWorld | Right at the street party, malls and BTS |
| **Silom / Sathorn** | One Bangkok (short ride), river | Good middle ground, more hotel choice |

## Countdown survival tips

- **Eat early.** Restaurants near the venues are full by evening.
- **Pick a meeting point** in case your group gets separated. Phone signal can drop in huge crowds.
- **Charge everything.** Photos, videos and maps drain your battery.
- **Don't plan an early flight** on 1 January.
- **It's school holidays in Malaysia**, so flights home around New Year are expensive. Book now.
