## Bangkok or Singapore?

| | Bangkok | Singapore |
|---|---|---|
| Getting there from KL | ~2-hour flight | ~1-hour flight, or 5–6 hours by bus |
| Hotels | Cheaper | Expensive, book early |
| Food and shopping | Cheaper, more to do | Pricier |
| Getting home after the show | Traffic is heavy, plan ahead | Easy MRT |

**Rule of thumb:** if you live in the south or want the easy option, choose Singapore. If you want a cheaper hotel and a holiday around the concert, choose Bangkok.

## Where to stay in Bangkok

### Rajamangala National Stadium (BTS, stadium shows)

Rajamangala is in the east of the city and **not on the BTS**. After a sold-out stadium show, Grab prices jump and taxis are hard to find. Stay somewhere with an easy ride home, or close to the Airport Rail Link (Ramkhamhaeng or Hua Mak), then take a short Grab.

## Where to stay in Singapore

The **National Stadium** and **Singapore Indoor Stadium** are both at Kallang, next to **Stadium MRT** on the Circle Line. Stay near the Circle Line, or in nearby Lavender or Bugis, for an easy ride back.

## Concert trip checklist

- [ ] **Tickets only from official sellers** (or the official resale platform)
- [ ] **Flights booked after tickets are confirmed.** Concert dates push up fares, so book soon after
- [ ] **Hotel with free cancellation**
- [ ] **Data:** a [Thailand eSIM](/blog/thailand-esim-for-malaysians/) or a regional eSIM so you can call a Grab after the show
- [ ] **Bangkok:** submit the free [TDAC arrival card](/blog/tdac-thailand-digital-arrival-card-malaysians/) within 3 days of arrival
- [ ] **Check the venue's bag rules.** Stadiums usually limit bag size and ban power banks over a certain capacity
