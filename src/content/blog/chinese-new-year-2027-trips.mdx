Not everyone balik kampung for Chinese New Year. Lots of Malaysian families do reunion dinner at home, then use the long weekend for a short trip. Others skip the crowds and go somewhere new.

The catch: everyone has the same idea. Flights and hotels for CNY week get expensive from December. Here are the dates and five trips that fit a short break.

## 1. Bangkok: Chinatown goes all out

Yaowarat, Bangkok's Chinatown, celebrates Chinese New Year in a big way, with red lanterns, lion dances and street food stalls along the road. Go in the evening, when the street is lit up.

Combine it with malls, markets and temples using our [3-day Bangkok itinerary](/blog/bangkok-3-day-itinerary/). Muslim travellers can check [halal food in Bangkok](/blog/halal-food-bangkok/) and [Muslim-friendly hotels](/blog/muslim-friendly-hotels-bangkok/).

## 2. Hatyai: the easy long-weekend trip

Hatyai is the classic short trip: great food, shopping and cheap hotels, and northern families can drive there. Expect long border queues at Bukit Kayu Hitam on the first and last day of the long weekend, so cross early in the morning.

Getting there: see [KL to Hatyai by car, train, bus or flight](/blog/kl-to-hatyai/) and [where to stay in Hatyai](/blog/where-to-stay-in-hatyai/). Every traveller, including kids, needs a free [TDAC arrival card](/blog/tdac-thailand-digital-arrival-card-malaysians/).

## 3. Singapore: no flight needed

Singapore's Chinatown is decorated for weeks before CNY, and the attractions stay open over the holidays. You can get there by bus, train, car or a 1-hour flight. See [KL to Singapore: every option compared](/blog/kl-to-singapore/).

Remember the free SG Arrival Card, and note that some shops and hawker stalls close on the first two days of CNY.

## 4. Chiang Mai: cool weather

February is still Chiang Mai's dry, cool season, with comfortable days and cool nights. It's a good choice if you want nature, cafés and night markets instead of crowds.

## Booking tips for CNY

1. **Book flights first.** They go up faster than hotels.
2. **Choose free cancellation** on hotels so you can lock in a price now and change later.
3. **Leave on the morning of Day 1 or Day 2** if your family does reunion dinner on CNY eve (Friday 5 February). Flights on those mornings are often cheaper than the day before.
4. **Avoid the last evening** of the long weekend for border crossings and airport queues.
