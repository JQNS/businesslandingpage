## What is HYROX?

HYROX is a standardised fitness race, run in the same format all over the world. That means your time in KL is directly comparable to London, Hong Kong or New York. Every race is:

**8 × 1 km runs**, each followed by one workout station:

| # | Station |
|---|---|
| 1 | SkiErg (1,000 m) |
| 2 | Sled Push |
| 3 | Sled Pull |
| 4 | Burpee Broad Jumps |
| 5 | Rowing (1,000 m) |
| 6 | Farmers Carry |
| 7 | Sandbag Lunges |
| 8 | Wall Balls |

**Divisions:** Open and Pro (heavier weights), raced Solo, as Doubles, or as a 4-person Relay. If it's your first race, **Open Doubles** is the friendliest way to start.

## Race-day tips from experienced HYROXers

1. **Pace the first run.** Everyone goes out too fast, and you'll pay for it at the sled push.
2. **Practise the Roxzone,** the transition area between running and stations. Time is won and lost there.
3. **Wall balls come last** and are where most people struggle. Break them into sets early.
4. **Bring:** grippy shoes, gloves for the sled pull (optional), electrolytes and a change of clothes.
5. **Hydrate the day before.** KL in December is hot and humid, even indoors.
6. **Arrive early** to collect your wristband and walk the course.
