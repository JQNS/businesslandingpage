## 1. Al Meroz Hotel: the fully halal choice

Al Meroz is known as one of Bangkok's first halal hotels. It has **halal restaurants and prayer rooms**, is styled like a mosque, and is near the Muslim community around Ramkhamhaeng. The trade-off is location: it's away from the BTS, so you'll rely on Grab or the Airport Rail Link (Ramkhamhaeng station).

## 2. Nouvo City Hotel: halal breakfast in the old town

A boutique hotel in Banglamphu, near Khao San Road and the Grand Palace. It serves a **halal breakfast**, its restaurant is reported to hold a halal rating, and staff can point you to mosques nearby. It's the best base for temples and the old town.

## 3. Le Méridien Bangkok: the upscale option

In Surawong, between Silom and Patpong, close to BTS Sala Daeng and MRT Sam Yan. The hotel is recognised by Crescent Rating and the Islamic Committee of Bangkok, and offers **halal-certified breakfast, room service and minibar items**.

## Best areas for Muslim travellers

| Area | Why | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Nana / Soi Arab** | Bangkok's biggest cluster of halal restaurants | Food lovers, budget trips |
| **Pratunam** | Halal food stalls and restaurants near the markets | Shoppers |
| **Banglamphu / Old Town** | Historic mosques and halal food near the temples | Sightseeing |
| **Ramkhamhaeng** | Established local Muslim community | A fully halal stay |

## Tips for a smooth stay

- **Ask for a prayer mat and qibla direction** when you book. Many hotels can provide them.
- **Pick room-only rates** at hotels without halal breakfast, and eat at a halal restaurant nearby instead.
- **Look for the Thai halal logo.** The green halal mark from the Central Islamic Council of Thailand appears on certified restaurants and packaged food, even in 7-Eleven.
- **Plan meals ahead** with our [halal food guide to Bangkok](/blog/halal-food-bangkok/).
