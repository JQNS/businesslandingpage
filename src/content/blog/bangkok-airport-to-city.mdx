Bangkok has **two airports**, and Malaysians land at both. Before anything else, check your booking:

- **DMK = Don Mueang**: AirAsia and most budget flights from KL, Penang and JB.
- **BKK = Suvarnabhumi**: Malaysia Airlines and most full-service airlines.

Here's the fastest, cheapest and least stressful way from each one to your hotel.

## Suvarnabhumi (BKK) to the city

| Option | Cost (approx.) | Time | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Airport Rail Link** | ~45 baht to Phaya Thai | ~26 min | Solo, light luggage, hotels near BTS |
| **Grab** | Varies by time and traffic | 30–60 min | Couples, late arrivals |
| **Metered taxi** | Meter + 50 baht airport fee + tolls | 30–60 min | Groups of 3–4 |
| **Pre-booked transfer** | Fixed price | 30–60 min | Families, lots of luggage |

**Airport Rail Link:** go down to the basement level (B) of the terminal. Trains run roughly from early morning to midnight, every 10–15 minutes. Change at **Makkasan** for the MRT (Phetchaburi station) or at **Phaya Thai** for the BTS. You can tap a contactless Visa or Mastercard at the gate instead of buying a token.

**Taxi:** use the official taxi queue on Level 1. Insist on the meter. Don't accept a fixed "flat price" from anyone who approaches you inside the terminal.

## Don Mueang (DMK) to the city

| Option | Cost (approx.) | Time | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SRT Red Line + MRT** | ~33 baht to Krung Thep Aphiwat, plus MRT fare | 40–60 min | Budget travellers going to MRT areas |
| **A1 bus to Mo Chit BTS** | Cheap, pay on board | 30–45 min | Hotels on the BTS Sukhumvit line |
| **Grab** | Varies by time and traffic | 30–60 min | Most travellers |
| **Pre-booked transfer** | Fixed price | 30–60 min | Families, night arrivals |

**Red Line train:** the station is connected to the terminal by a walkway. The Red Line doesn't reach central areas like Siam or Sukhumvit, so you'll change to the **MRT Blue Line** at Krung Thep Aphiwat (Bang Sue).

<Callout type="tip" title="Landing on a late AirAsia flight?">
Trains stop around midnight, and the taxi queue can be long when several flights land together. Pre-book a transfer, or request a Grab as soon as you clear immigration.
</Callout>

## Pre-book a transfer (recommended for families)

A driver waits in the arrival hall with your name, the price is fixed, and nobody haggles. It's the least stressful option after a flight with kids or big bags.

## Before you land

- **Data:** install a [Thailand eSIM](/blog/thailand-esim-for-malaysians/) at home so Grab works the moment you land.
- **TDAC:** submit your free [arrival card](/blog/tdac-thailand-digital-arrival-card-malaysians/) within 3 days before arrival.
- **Cash:** have a few hundred baht for the bus, a taxi or a snack. Airport exchange rates are usually worse than in the city.
