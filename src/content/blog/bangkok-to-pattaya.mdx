Pattaya is the easiest beach escape from Bangkok: about 150 km down the highway, roughly 2 hours by road. There's no flight to worry about and no ferry. The real choice is between **cheap and slightly fiddly** (the bus) and **door to door** (a taxi or private transfer).

## Quick comparison

| | Bus | Grab / taxi | Private transfer (booked ahead) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | ~฿150 per person | ~฿1,200–1,500 + tolls per car | Fixed price per car |
| Door to door | ❌ Bus station to bus station | ✅ | ✅ |
| Luggage | 1–2 bags | Depends on the car | Choose a van for big groups |
| Best for | Solo travellers, couples | Short-notice trips | Families, groups, airport arrivals |

Rates in this guide use roughly **RM1 ≈ ฿8**. Check the rate on the day.

## Option 1: The bus (cheapest)

**From central Bangkok: Ekkamai bus terminal (Eastern Bus Terminal)**

- Right next to **BTS Ekkamai**, so it's the easiest station if you're staying on Sukhumvit.
- Buses to Pattaya run about **every hour, roughly 05:00 to 22:00**, for around **฿150**.
- The ride takes 2–2.5 hours. Buses drop you at the Pattaya bus terminal on North Pattaya Road. From there, take a songthaew (the shared pickup trucks) or a Grab to your hotel.

**From the north of Bangkok: Mo Chit 2 (Northern Bus Terminal)** has buses too, for a few baht more. Use it if you're staying near Chatuchak or arriving from northern Thailand.

<Callout type="tip" title="Going on a Friday or for an event?">
Buy your seat in advance or arrive early. Friday evenings, long weekends and big events like [Tomorrowland Thailand](/blog/tomorrowland-thailand-2026-guide/) fill buses fast, and the ticket queue at Ekkamai can get long.
</Callout>

## Option 2: Straight from the airport

**From Suvarnabhumi (BKK):** bus **389** leaves from **Level 1, Gate 8** and runs to Pattaya and Jomtien for roughly **฿130–160**, about once an hour. Bags are limited (around 2 bags, up to 20 kg each).

**From Don Mueang (DMK)**, where most AirAsia flights from Malaysia land, there's no simple direct bus. Your options:

- Grab or a taxi to Ekkamai or Mo Chit, then the bus (cheap, but slow with luggage)
- A **private transfer straight to Pattaya**, which is the easiest choice after a flight

## Option 3: Grab or taxi

Grab works for Bangkok to Pattaya, and so do regular taxis. Expect roughly **฿1,200–1,500 plus tolls** for a standard car. With metered taxis, agree on a total price before you set off. It's quick and comfortable, but between three or four people a pre-booked transfer usually works out similar, without the haggling.

## Option 4: Private transfer (best for groups and families)

A pre-booked private car or van picks you up from your Bangkok hotel or the airport and drops you at your Pattaya hotel. **The price is per car, not per person**, so split four ways it becomes good value.

## What about the train?

There is a train from Bangkok to Pattaya. It's very cheap, but there are only a few departures, it's slow, and the Pattaya station is outside the centre. It's fun if you love trains. For everyone else, the bus is faster and simpler.

## Where to stay in Pattaya

- **Central Pattaya / Beach Road:** walk to the beach, malls and Walking Street. Best for first-timers.
- **Jomtien:** quieter, more relaxed, better for families.
- **Naklua / North Pattaya:** calmer resorts, close to the bus terminal.
