# Trust-Wise social posts: Threads first, then X

Ready to copy and paste. Every post sends people to a guide on trust-wise.org.

## How to post

- **The first line is everything.** It's the only part people see before deciding to scroll past, so never change the hook lines when you edit.
- **Don't give the whole answer in the post.** Give 70% of it and let the guide finish it. That gap is what gets the click.
- **Link goes in your first reply** ("Full guide here 👇 [link]"), not in the post. Threads and X both show link posts to fewer people.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Reach depends on it more than anything else. A reply like "Yes! Bukit Kayu Hitam is covered in the guide 👇 [link]" also gets clicks.
- **1–2 posts a day.** Post the dated ones (🔥) first because they stop working after the date passes. Mix in the evergreen (🌿) and bait-for-replies (💬) posts.
- **Best times (MYT):** 8–9am, 12–1pm, 9–11pm.
- **Topic tags:** Threads allows one per post. On X, use 1–2 hashtags at most.
- **Keep every claim true.** All hooks below match what the guides say. If a hook promises something the guide doesn't deliver, people unfollow and Threads shows your posts to fewer people.

Link pattern (use `utm_source=x` on X):
`https://trust-wise.org/blog/<slug>/?utm_source=threads&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<slug>`
